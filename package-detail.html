About This Trip

Known as the Scotland of the East, Meghalaya is a paradise of rolling green hills, spectacular waterfalls, mysterious caves, and crystal-clear rivers. This carefully curated 6-day journey takes you through the most breathtaking landscapes of this enchanting state.

Our Meghalaya Explorer tour offers an immersive look into local Khasi culture. You will visit majestic living root bridges constructed centuries ago, cruise on the transparent waters of the Dawki river, witness the awe-inspiring rainstorms of Cherrapunji, and walk through Asia's cleanest village.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Guwahati & Transfer to Shillong

Fly into Guwahati, scenic drive to Shillong, evening walk at Police Bazaar, overnight Shillong.

Day 2

Shillong Sightseeing

Elephant Falls, Don Bosco Museum, Ward's Lake, Shillong Peak, Cathedral Catholic Church, local Khasi cuisine.

Day 3

Cherrapunji — The Wettest Place on Earth

Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai Cave, Eco Park, rain & mist experience.

Day 4

Living Root Bridges & Nongriat

Trek to double-decker living root bridge, natural swimming pools, Rainbow Falls, village life.

Day 5

Dawki & Mawlynnong

Crystal-clear Umngot River boating, Asia's cleanest village Mawlynnong, living root bridge at Riwai.

Day 6

Departure

Morning at Umiam Lake (Barapani), transfer to Guwahati airport, departure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Best time to visit Meghalaya?

The best time to visit Meghalaya is between October and June when the weather is pleasant.

How difficult is the trek to living root bridges?

It involves descending and ascending about 3500 steep steps. It requires a moderate to good level of fitness.

Do I need any permits?

Indian nationals do not need Inner Line Permits for Meghalaya. Foreign nationals require a valid Indian visa.

What should I pack?

Pack comfortable trekking shoes, an umbrella or raincoat, light cottons for summer, and warm layers for the evening.

Is it safe for solo travelers?

Yes, Meghalaya is considered one of the safest states in India for travelers, including solo female travelers.
