Born in the Heart of the Northeast

GT Travels was founded in 2016 by a group of passionate travelers from Shillong, Meghalaya, who believed that Northeast India deserved to be on every traveler's bucket list. Having grown up exploring the misty hills of Meghalaya, the monasteries of Tawang, and the wildlife of Kaziranga, we turned our lifelong love for this region into a mission — to share its magic with the world.

We specialize in crafting personalized itineraries that go beyond typical tourist trails. By partnering with local guides and homestays, we ensure our tours support local communities and promote sustainable tourism across the Seven Sisters and Sikkim.

Why Travel With Us

Our core principles for organizing trips to the Northeast.

Local Expertise

Our team comprises native Northeast experts who know the hidden trails, seasonal nuances, and deep cultural histories of the region.

We partner directly with indigenous communities, prioritizing eco-friendly homestays and promoting leaving-no-trace travel.

Authentic Experiences

From tasting traditional Mizo cuisine to attending the vibrant Hornbill Festival, we ensure your experiences are culturally enriching.
